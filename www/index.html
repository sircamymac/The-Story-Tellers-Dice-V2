How the Dice Work

6 8 10 12

1Roll the dice

Six dice build a story seed: plot hook, villain motive, plot twist, complication, location and reward. Tap a single die to roll it, or press Roll all dice to roll them all at once.

2Lock and reroll

Love a result? Press Lock on that die and it will stay put while you roll the rest. Reroll redoes just one die, and Lock all or Unlock all do what they say. Open See the full table on any die to read every possible result. Each die has a fixed size (d20, d12, d10, d8, d10 and d8), but its table holds many more entries. Switch entries on or off to choose which ones the die can roll, up to the size of the die. Press × twice to remove an entry for good, and Restore removed brings built-in entries back. Add yours writes your own entries into a table, and your choices are kept for that theme.

3Read your seed

Your rolled results gather in the story seed box below the dice, where the wizard conjures them for you.

4Add your own notes

Use the Notes box under the seed to write names, ideas and twists of your own. Press Save notes to add them to the seed in your book, or just press Save seed to save everything together.

5Save to the seed book

Press Save seed, give it a name, and it joins My Seeds. Open the book to flip through your tales with the Prev and Next buttons or the arrow keys, or tap an entry on the contents page to jump straight to it.

6Open a seed full screen

On any page of the book, press Open to see that seed full screen. Edit its title, write more notes, then press Save changes. On the page itself, the pen button renames, the bin deletes (press twice to confirm) and Copy copies the seed with its notes.

7Wandering Wordsmith

Stuck for a description? Pick a scene type near the bottom of the page and press Draw words for fresh phrases to borrow.

8Battle Maps

Use the Battle Maps tab to generate a random map. Pick a size and a biome, then fill it using the token sidebar: choose from the library, make a Random token, Draw your own or Upload an image, press Add token to place one or Scatter to drop your selection across the map, then drag tokens anywhere. Export saves a PNG or JPEG. Save map keeps it in the map library, optionally attached to a tale: open the book and press Maps, or press View battle maps inside a tale.

9Themes and reset

The Theme menu changes the tables, words, colors and scenery. You can switch themes between rolls, and this scroll changes with them. The ↻ button clears the dice and your notes, and the ? button brings this scroll back any time.
